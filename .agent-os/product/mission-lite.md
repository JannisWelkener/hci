# Product Mission (Lite)

HCI Hub is a comprehensive web application that helps HCI and UX Design students plan, execute, and document human-centered design projects by providing structured methodological support, templates, and AI-assisted tools aligned with ISO 9241-210 standards.

HCI Hub serves master's students and academic teams working on semester-long HCI projects who need systematic guidance through the Human-Centered Design process. Unlike generic project management tools, HCI Hub is specifically built around ISO 9241-210 standards and provides interconnected documentation templates (personas, user stories, journey maps, prototypes) with AI-enhanced learning support for method selection, content generation, and quality assurance.
